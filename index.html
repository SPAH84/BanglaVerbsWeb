Quick Start Guide

💡 How It Works

BanglaVerbs builds speech reflexes through active listening & recall across the 4 foundational Bangla pronouns:

আমি (Ami) I
তুমি (Tumi) You (familiar)
আপনি (Apni) You (polite)
সে (Se) He / She (ordinary)

🎧 Drill Mode Cycle

  1. English Prompt: Speaks the English verb (e.g. "To do").
  2. Recall Pause: Silent gap for you to say the Bangla verb aloud.
  3. Bangla Infinitive: Speaks the base verb (করা).
  4. Conjugations: Automatically speaks each person in order (আমি করি, তুমি করো, etc.) with live screen highlights.
  5. Advance: Moves hands-free to the next verb in your chosen set.

⚙️ Controls & Settings

  • Tense: Switch between Pr. Simple, Pr. Perfect, Past Simple, and Past Perfect.
  • Level: Choose Set 1 (20 basic), Set 2 (40 extended), or All (60 verbs).
  • Voice: Toggle between Natural Female and Male voices in Playback Settings.
  • Speed: Adjust to 0.8x, 1.0x, 1.2x, or 1.4x without pitch distortion.

📱 Practice Screens

  • Drill: Hands-free audio repetition player.
  • Grid: Interactive verb table. Tap any card or conjugation to hear instant pronunciation!
  • Quiz: 10-question multiple-choice challenges to test your recall.
